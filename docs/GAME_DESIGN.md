# Game design

**How to Hunt** is a first-person, physics-based hunting and progression game with a cartoony, low-poly look.

## Setting
The player is stranded on an isolated mountain island: a high plateau ringed by cliffs, with sea or clouds far below. The island is split into zones going up the mountain. Higher player level unlocks tougher zones. Zone bosses and quests unlock new areas.

## Core loop
Hunt, collect loot, sell it, upgrade, reach tougher animals, repeat. There is always a bigger tool to buy and a bigger animal to hunt.

## Animals
Data-driven, in three movement types:
- **Walking:** rabbits, goats, boars, deer, bears, mammoths, up to large dinosaurs.
- **Crawling:** snakes, lizards, giant beetles, spiders, giant centipedes.
- **Flying:** birds, bats, eagles, giant dragonflies, pteranodons.

Each animal has a tier, level requirement, HP, speed, behavior (flee, ignore, stalk, charge), weak spots, loot, sell value and a rare variant with a low spawn chance and a high price. A collection log tracks every animal and variant.

## Tools
Tiered from slingshot to absurd late-game gear: bow, crossbow, rifle and beyond, plus traps, bait, binoculars, climbing gear, grappling hook and armor. Stats: damage, range, reload, noise, durability, price. Trick shots (headshot, long distance, mid-air, ricochet) pay bonus money.

## Progression
Player level and XP, a skill tree (aim, stamina, carry capacity, stealth, tracking, sell price), quests, zone bosses, unlockable areas, and an optional minigame using only in-game money.

## Tone
Cartoon violence only: no gore, no blood spray (poofs, stars, confetti). Teen-rated content. No real-money gambling or loot boxes.

## Originality
The loop is inspired in general terms by "start with nothing, earn money, buy better gear". No code, art, audio, text, names or characters are taken from any other game.

## Co-op
1 to 4 players via Steam lobbies. Shared island and money, separate inventories.
